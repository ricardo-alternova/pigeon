## Accounts

All access goes through Executor. Find each `accountId` with Executor `search` (see `executor-verbs.md`). App is `gmail-api` or `outlook-mail-api`.

`In all` = yes means rules with Scope `all` apply to this account. Accounts with `no` get only the rules that name them.
